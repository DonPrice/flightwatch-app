Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

✅ The short version

FlightWatch does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

1. Overview

FlightWatch ("the app") is developed by Donovan Price for use by airport transfer drivers. This privacy policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

FlightWatch is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app does not operate any servers, does not create user accounts, and does not transmit personal information to any party other than the flight data API described below.

2. Calendar Access

FlightWatch requests full access to your device's calendar in order to:

  • Read event titles, notes, and locations to detect flight numbers
  • Display booking details (client name, address, phone) within the app
  • Monitor changes to calendar events in real time

Your calendar data is never transmitted outside your device. It is read locally and used only to display information within the app. No calendar content is sent to any server operated by the app developer.

3. Flight Data — FlightAware AeroAPI

To look up live flight status, FlightWatch sends flight numbers (e.g. QF404) to the FlightAware AeroAPI — a third-party aviation data service. The only information sent is the flight identifier and a date window.

No personal information is included in these requests. Client names, phone numbers, addresses, and calendar notes are never sent to FlightAware or any other service.

FlightAware's privacy policy is available at flightaware.com/about/privacy.

The FlightAware API key you enter in Settings is stored only in your device's local storage (UserDefaults) and is never transmitted to any party other than FlightAware's API endpoint.

4. Notifications

FlightWatch uses Apple's local notification system to deliver alerts about flight status changes, reminders, and delays. All notifications are generated on your device. No notification content is sent to any external server.

5. Data Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults and standard app storage:

  • Your selected calendar identifier
  • Your FlightAware API key
  • Cached flight status data (to persist between app launches)

This data is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app. It is not backed up to iCloud or any other cloud service.

6. Third-Party Services

FlightWatch uses one third-party service:

  • FlightAware AeroAPI — receives flight identifiers only (no personal data). See their privacy policy at flightaware.com/about/privacy.

The app does not use analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or any other third-party SDKs.

7. Children's Privacy

FlightWatch is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
